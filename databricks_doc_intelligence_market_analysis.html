White space ✦
Your accelerator opportunity
Full-stack IDP + industry schemas + Genie + Lakebase. No direct competitor occupies this space today.
Market white space — what doesn't exist yet
Industry schema templates
No accelerator ships pre-built extraction schemas for FS (loan docs, KYC), healthcare (clinical notes, EOB), or legal (contracts, filings).
Genie-first document Q&A
No marketplace solution wires parsed documents into AI/BI Genie for natural language querying out of the box.
Lakebase operational layer
Lakebase (Neon/Postgres) is brand new. No accelerator uses it as the transactional serving layer for document metadata + state.
End-to-end governed pipeline
Existing solutions stop at extraction. No one packages ingestion → parse → extract → classify → lineage → serve as a governed Unity Catalog asset.
Multi-modal document support
Tables, figures, diagrams, stamps — most accelerators only handle text PDFs. ai_parse_document handles all formats but no ISV wraps it end-to-end.
Marketplace-ready pricing model
No ISV sells a per-document or consumption-based IDP accelerator on Databricks Marketplace with tiered industry bundles.

Key strategic signals
Databricks' ai_parse_document (Nov 2025) is 3–5x cheaper than Azure/AWS equivalents — removes the biggest cost objection to enterprise IDP at scale.
Lakebase (Postgres OLTP, 2025) enables a transactional document registry alongside analytical Delta Lake — unprecedented in a single platform.
Databricks has partnerships with Anthropic ($100M, Mar 2025), Gemini ($, Jun 2025) and OpenAI ($100M, Sep 2025) — model-agnostic extraction is viable and differentiating.
The Databricks Marketplace has 1,200+ listings but no end-to-end IDP accelerator with industry schema bundles — a genuine first-mover gap.
79% of enterprises now use GenAI in at least one function (McKinsey 2025) — document extraction is the highest-ROI entry point for most regulated industries.
